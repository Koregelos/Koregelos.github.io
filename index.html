Strategy Robustness Testing

2024 – Present

A strategy that looks great once can still be a fluke, so I put every one through robustness testing to check that its results were not luck. Many of my early strategies failed and were scrapped or rebuilt. The real exam comes after publishing, on prices the strategy has never seen. I released this one in September 2024 with ten assets named up front and have not changed it since. It has beaten simply holding on seven of the ten, by an average of 34 percentage points, and on Ethereum it is up 36% while holding is down 14%.

Universal Strategy Asset Rotation System

Nov 2024 – Present

A trading system I designed and coded from scratch in Pine Script. Every day it compares five cryptocurrencies head to head, holds the strongest, and steps aside to cash when the whole market is falling. The two years since I published it have been rough for crypto, which is the test that matters: tested on daily prices with trading fees included, the system has outperformed holding Bitcoin by 17 percentage points, with a worst drop a third smaller. Its cash rule alone was worth 40 points.
