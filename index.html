Universal Strategy Asset Rotation System

Nov 2024 – Jan 2025

A trading system I designed and wrote in Pine Script. Each day it compares five cryptocurrencies head to head, holds the one that wins every matchup, and moves to cash when the whole market is trending down. Its engine is my Universal Strategy, which averages six community-built trend indicators into a single buy or sell score and is packaged as a library so the system can reuse it on every pair. The script also calculates its own Sharpe, Sortino, and Omega ratios with trading fees included. All figures are backtests, not live results.
