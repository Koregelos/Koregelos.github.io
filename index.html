Strategy Robustness Testing

2024 – Present

A strategy that looks great once can still be a fluke. Trading Strategy Research

2023 – Present

One good result can be luck. This takes a strategy's real week-by-week backtest results and reshuffles them into 200 alternate histories, live in your browser, to measure how often it still wins and how deep the worst stretch gets. Press Rerun for a new set. It measures luck in the order of events, not whether the strategy keeps working. Behind it sit more than 100 strategies I built and backtested in Python and Pine Script, and an automated trading system with built-in risk management.

Universal Strategy Asset Rotation System

Nov 2024 – Present

A trading system I designed and coded from scratch in Pine Script. Every day it compares five cryptocurrencies head to head, holds the strongest, and steps aside to cash when the whole market is falling. The two years since I published it have been rough for crypto, which is the test that matters: the system is down 2% where holding Bitcoin is down 19%, and the same system without its cash rule would have lost 42%. Figures are a re-run of the published code on daily prices with 0.5% trading fees, not live trading.
